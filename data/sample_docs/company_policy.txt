Employee Leave Policy - GrezityComply Pvt Ltd

All full-time employees are entitled to 18 paid leaves per calendar year,
consisting of 12 casual/sick leaves and 6 earned leaves.

Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working
days in advance, except in emergencies. Sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive
days requires a medical certificate.

Unused earned leaves can be carried forward to the next year, up to a
maximum of 15 days. Casual/sick leaves do not carry forward and lapse at
the end of the calendar year.

Employees on probation (first 90 days) are eligible for a maximum of 2
paid leaves. Any additional leave during probation will be treated as
leave without pay (LWP).

Remote employees follow the same leave policy as on-site employees. Leave
approval is subject to manager sign-off in the HR portal.
